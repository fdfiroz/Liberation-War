Voices of Freedom

Featured Interviews

Hear firsthand accounts from those who lived through and fought in the Liberation War.

Abdul Karim

Freedom Fighter — Sector 2

We fought not just for land, but for our language, our identity, and the right to live with dignity as a free nation.

A brave veteran of Sector 2, Abdul Karim joined the Mukti Bahini at the age of 19 and participated in several key operations during the war.

45 min Listen

Fatema Begum

Civilian Witness

The night the army came, everything changed. We lost our home, but we never lost hope for our beloved Bangladesh.

Fatema Begum survived the 1971 crackdown in Dhaka and became a refugee. Her testimony provides crucial civilian perspective on the war.

38 min Listen

Major Rafiq Uddin

Military Commander

Every sector, every village, every person contributed. This was not one man's war — it was a people's revolution.

Major Rafiq served as a sector commander and led guerrilla operations in the northern regions of Bangladesh during the war.
