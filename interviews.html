Voices of Liberation

Firsthand accounts from freedom fighters, civilians, and leaders who shaped the course of Bangladesh's independence.

Abdul Karim

Freedom Fighter — Sector 2

We fought not just for land, but for our language, our identity, and the right to live with dignity as a free nation.

Abdul Karim joined the Mukti Bahini at the age of 19. He participated in several key guerrilla operations in the Comilla and Noakhali regions during the Liberation War. After independence, he dedicated his life to documenting the stories of fellow fighters.

45 minutes Recorded: January 2015

Fatema Begum

Civilian Witness — Dhaka

The night the army came, everything changed. We lost our home, but we never lost hope for our beloved Bangladesh.

Fatema Begum was 24 years old during Operation Searchlight. She witnessed the initial crackdown in Dhaka and fled with her family to the Indian border. Her testimony provides a crucial civilian perspective on the displacement and suffering of ordinary Bangladeshis.

38 minutes Recorded: March 2016

Major Rafiq Uddin

Military Commander — Sector 7

Every sector, every village, every person contributed. This was not one man's war — it was a people's revolution.

Major Rafiq served as a sector commander leading guerrilla operations in the northern regions. A trained military officer, he defected from the Pakistani army in March 1971 and organized one of the most effective resistance units.

52 minutes Recorded: December 2014

Dr. Kamal Hossain

Political Leader & Constitutional Architect

The constitution we drafted was not just a legal document — it was the embodiment of the aspirations and sacrifices of 75 million people.

Dr. Kamal Hossain was one of the principal architects of the Constitution of Bangladesh. He served in key political roles during and after the Liberation War, and was instrumental in drafting the legal framework of the new nation.

63 minutes Recorded: June 2017

Shamsul Huda

War Correspondent & Journalist

My pen became my weapon. Every word I wrote was a testament to the courage I witnessed on those blood-soaked fields.

Shamsul Huda was one of the few journalists who remained in Dhaka during the crackdown. He secretly documented atrocities and smuggled reports to international media, helping to bring global attention to the crisis.

41 minutes Recorded: August 2018

Taramon Bibi

Freedom Fighter — Narsingdi

They said women cannot fight. But I picked up a rifle and proved them wrong. Freedom knows no gender.

Taramon Bibi is one of the most celebrated female freedom fighters of Bangladesh. She actively participated in combat operations and was awarded the Bir Protik, one of the highest gallantry awards for bravery during the Liberation War.

35 minutes Recorded: February 2019
